Delete your FreshStart account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open FreshStart and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete everything to confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account, your profile, meals, weigh-ins, kitchen list and all other data, both in the cloud and on your phone.

Without the app

Email the developer at the address on FreshStart's Google Play listing, from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my FreshStart account". Your account and all its data will be deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a confirmation.

What is kept

Nothing. No copies of your data are kept after deletion. Data you shared to Health Connect stays in Health Connect on your phone, where you can delete it in Health Connect's settings.
