FreshStart privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

FreshStart helps you lose weight by tracking meals, weight and habits. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. FreshStart shows no ads and never sells or shares your data for advertising.

Data you enter

Your profile (name if you give one, sex, age, height, weight, goal and activity level), the meals and foods you log, your weigh-ins, your kitchen list, and which lessons you've read.

  • Without an account, this data is stored only on your phone.
  • With an account, it is also stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so you can restore it on another phone. Only you can access it: it is tied to your sign-in and protected by access rules. It is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google.

Account information

If you create an account, we use Firebase Authentication to store your email address, or your Google account's email and name if you sign in with Google. This is used only to sign you in.

Health Connect

If you choose to connect Health Connect, FreshStart reads your weight, steps and active calories, and writes the weigh-ins and meal calories you log. Health Connect data is used only to show your progress and activity in the app. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never sent to our servers. Use of data received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Photo scanning (optional)

If you add your own Google Gemini API key, photos you choose to scan, your kitchen list when you ask for meal ideas, and a summary of your log (profile, weigh-ins, foods, calories and steps) when you ask the AI coach for tips, are sent directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API to identify foods, suggest meals and write coaching tips. Your key is stored only on your phone. Google's handling of that data is covered by Google's Gemini API terms. If you don't add a key, nothing is sent to Google.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder data leaves your device.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your account data is kept until you delete it. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account. See how to delete your account, including if you no longer have the app. Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on your phone.

Children

FreshStart is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Medical disclaimer

FreshStart is a general wellness tool and does not give medical advice. Talk to your doctor before making major changes to your diet or exercise.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact the developer using the email address on FreshStart's Google Play listing.
